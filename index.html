Privacy Policy for Anime Figure Tracker

Effective Date: August 29, 2026

Anime Figure Tracker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Collection Data

You may add information about anime figures in your collection, including figure name, series, character, manufacturer, product line, scale, condition, shelf location, notes, price paid, ownership status, and release information.

This collection data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your full personal collection to our servers.
