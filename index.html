Privacy Policy

Anime Figure Tracker — Last updated August 2026

Anime Figure Tracker ("AFT", "we", "the app") is a personal collection tracking tool. Your collection data is yours. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising.

Data stored on your device and iCloud

Your figure collection — names, series, photos, prices, notes — is stored exclusively in Apple's iCloud using SwiftData with CloudKit. It syncs across your own devices and is governed by Apple's iCloud Terms of Service. We never see, access, or store this data on our servers.

Camera

The app uses your camera to scan barcodes and read text printed on figure boxes. The live camera feed is processed entirely on-device. When you tap "Scan box", a single still image is captured and sent to our catalog service solely to identify the figure. This image is used to return a result and is not retained by our servers.

Catalog service

When scanning a box, the app sends the barcode (JAN/EAN), any text read from the box, and a random anonymous device identifier to our catalog service. This identifier is a random UUID generated at install time. It is not the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), is not linked to your Apple ID, your name, or any personal information, and is regenerated if you delete and reinstall the app. Its only purpose is to require two independent devices before an unverified catalog entry is promoted, preventing spam.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or Apple ID.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs of any kind.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not access your photo library (photos are taken in-app only).

Data retention

Your collection data lives in iCloud and is controlled entirely by you. You can delete it at any time by deleting figures in the app or by managing iCloud storage in your device Settings. The anonymous device identifier stored in your device's local storage is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
