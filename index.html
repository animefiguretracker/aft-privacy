Privacy Policy for Anime Figure Tracker

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

Anime Figure Tracker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Collection Data

You may add information about anime figures in your collection, including figure name, series, character, manufacturer, product line, scale, condition, shelf location, notes, price paid, ownership status, release information, featured status, featured position, and your personal display preferences.

This collection data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your full personal collection to our servers.

Collection Sharing

The App may allow you to create and share a representation of your collection or selected figures using the iOS sharing tools.

Sharing is optional and begins only when you choose to use the sharing feature. The destination you select, such as Messages, Mail, or another installed app, may process the shared content according to its own privacy policy.

Photos You Add

You may add photos of figures to your local collection. These photos are stored on your device unless you choose to submit a photo for community catalog review.

When you use a photo to identify a figure, a reduced copy may be sent to our backend and AI provider to process that request. We do not retain the identification image unless you separately submit it as a community photo.

Optional Community Photo Submissions

If you choose to share a photo for catalog review, the App uploads that photo to our server along with related catalog information, such as figure name, series, character, manufacturer, product line, JAN or barcode if available, catalog ID if available, submission time, and a device identifier.

Community photo submission is optional. It only happens when you opt in.

Optional AI Background Cleanup

When you choose to submit a photo for community catalog review, you may choose to use AI background cleanup. This feature uses on-device image processing to place the subject of the photo on a white background before upload.

The cleanup feature is optional. If you do not enable it, the App uploads the review copy without AI background cleanup. Your saved local collection photo is not changed by this feature.

Catalog Search

When you search the figure catalog, the App sends your search terms to our backend so it can retrieve matching catalog records. Search terms may include a character, series, manufacturer, product line, or figure name.

Catalog searches are used to provide search results and may also be processed in limited server logs for security, debugging, abuse prevention, and service reliability.

Community Aura Ratings

If you submit a Community Aura rating, the App sends the catalog identifier, your selected rating from 1 to 5, and a random device identifier to our backend.

We use this information to calculate and display aggregate community ratings and to limit each App installation to one current rating per catalog figure.

Your individual rating is not displayed publicly. Other users may see the combined average rating and the total number of submitted ratings for a figure.

You may change your rating. Submitting another rating for the same catalog figure from the same App installation replaces your previous rating for that figure.

The App currently does not require an account for Community Aura ratings. Ratings are associated with a random installation identifier rather than your name, email address, Apple ID, or advertising identifier.

Device Identifier

The App may create a random device identifier. This identifier is used to support catalog features, prevent duplicate confirmations and Community Aura ratings, track community photo submissions, and count approved community photo contributions.

Before a Community Aura rating is stored on our server, the device identifier is transformed using a one-way cryptographic hash. This helps prevent duplicate ratings without storing the original identifier with the rating.

This identifier is not Apple’s advertising identifier and is not intended to directly identify you personally. If you delete and reinstall the App, this identifier may change.

Camera and Photo Library Access

The App may request access to your camera or photo library so you can scan figure boxes, identify figures, add photos to your collection, or submit photos for catalog review.

Camera and photo library access is used only for App features you choose to use.

Purchases and Subscription Status

If you purchase or subscribe to Anime Figure Tracker Pro, Apple processes the transaction. The App may receive information needed to determine whether your account has Pro access, such as purchase status, entitlement status, and subscription state.

We do not receive your full payment card information from Apple.

Advertising and Consent Information

The App may show ads to free users. Advertising may be provided through Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform.

Depending on your region and consent choices, advertising providers may collect or process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, and consent status. You may be asked to provide consent for personalized ads where required by law.

Diagnostics and Technical Information

The App or its service providers may process limited technical information needed to operate, debug, secure, and improve the App and backend services. This may include request timestamps, basic server logs, error information, and device or app environment details.

  • Approve or reject submitted community photos.

3. Community Photo Review and Display

If you opt in to community photo sharing, your submitted photo is uploaded for manual review.

Submitted photos may be approved or rejected. If approved, the App may show your photo as a community catalog photo for the matching figure or product. Other users may see approved community catalog photos when they find or view that figure.

If rejected, the App may show you a rejection message explaining that the photo was not approved and suggesting what kind of photo may be submitted instead.

Approval or rejection messages may be shown in the App. These messages are intended to be informational and related only to catalog photo review.

Please only submit photos that you took yourself or otherwise have the rights to share. Do not submit photos copied from stores, manufacturers, social media, other collectors, websites, databases, or search results unless you have permission.

Do not submit photos containing private information, unrelated people, offensive content, explicit content, or anything you do not want reviewed by us.

4. Revoking a Community Photo Submission

You may revoke a catalog photo submission from within the App.

When you revoke a submission, the App sends a request to remove the submitted community catalog photo from our server. Your local figure entry and local photo remain saved on your device unless you delete them yourself.

If a revoked photo had been approved, it should no longer be used as a community catalog image after the revocation is processed.

5. What Happens If You Delete the App

Deleting Anime Figure Tracker from your device generally removes your local collection and local photos, subject to Apple iOS behavior and any device backups you may have.

Deleting the App does not automatically notify our server to delete community photos or Community Aura ratings that you previously submitted.

If you want a previously submitted community photo removed from our server, please revoke it from the App before deleting the App or contact us using the email address below.

You may contact us to request deletion of a Community Aura rating where applicable. We may need information from your current App installation to locate the rating.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal collection data.

We may share or process information with service providers that help operate the App, including:

  • Cloudflare, for backend hosting, database storage, security, and server infrastructure.
  • Apple, for App Store purchases, subscriptions, sharing tools, and platform services.
  • Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform, for advertising and consent management.
  • AI, search, barcode, or catalog lookup providers used by our backend to help identify figures or improve catalog results.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Approved community catalog photos may be visible to other App users.

Aggregate Community Aura ratings and rating counts may be visible to other App users. We do not publicly display the device identifier or an individual user’s rating.

If you choose to share collection content through the iOS sharing tools, the content is sent to the destination you select.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, security needs, fraud prevention, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of the App, users, or others.

7. Advertising

Free users may see ads in the App.

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and applicable law.

Advertising partners may collect or receive information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and consent signals.

You can manage ad personalization through iOS settings and, where available, through consent prompts shown in the App.

8. Data Retention

Local collection data and local photos remain on your device until you delete them, delete the figure entry, or delete the App.

Community photo submissions may be stored on our server while pending review, after approval, or after rejection.

Approved community photos may remain stored and displayed until you revoke the submission, request deletion, or we remove them.

Rejected photos may be retained for a limited time for moderation, audit, abuse prevention, or operational purposes, unless deletion is requested and we are legally or operationally able to delete them.

Community Aura ratings may remain stored while the rating feature is offered. Submitting another rating for the same catalog figure from the same App installation replaces the previous rating.

You may contact us to request deletion of a Community Aura rating where applicable, although we may need information from your current App installation to locate the rating.

Server logs and technical records may be retained for security, debugging, analytics, abuse prevention, and service reliability.
