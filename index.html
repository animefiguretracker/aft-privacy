Anime Figure Tracker Privacy Policy Effective date: August 28, 2026 Anime Figure Tracker (“AFT,” “we,” or “us”) helps you catalog and track your anime figure collection. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used. Information You Provide AFT may store information you enter about your collection, including figure names, series, characters, manufacturers, prices, notes, shelf names, ownership status, condition, wishlist status, preorder status, and photos you choose to add. Collection data is stored on your device. If iCloud is available and enabled for the app, your collection may sync through Apple’s iCloud services. We do not operate or control Apple’s iCloud infrastructure. Photos, Scans, and Catalog Lookup If you use box scanning, photo lookup, barcode lookup, OCR, or manual catalog submission features, AFT may send the relevant image, barcode, recognized text, figure details, and a random app-generated device identifier to our backend so we can return catalog matches, improve matching quality, and prevent duplicate catalog confirmations. The random device identifier is not Apple’s advertising identifier and is not linked to an AFT account. Purchases AFT Pro subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information. Advertising The free version of AFT may show ads using Google Mobile Ads / AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK may collect information such as IP address, device identifiers, advertising data, app performance data, diagnostic data, crash data, and ad or app interaction data. This data may be used by Google to provide, measure, improve, and personalize advertising, subject to Google’s policies and your device settings. You can manage ad tracking and privacy preferences through iOS Settings where available. Analytics and Diagnostics AFT may use basic diagnostic information to understand crashes, reliability, and app performance. We use this information to maintain and improve the app. Data We Do Not Collect AFT does not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, health data, or payment card information through the app. Data Sharing We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers only as needed to operate app features, including catalog lookup, image scanning, app infrastructure, App Store purchases, iCloud sync, and advertising. Data Retention Data stored locally remains on your device unless you delete it or uninstall the app. Data synced through iCloud is managed through Apple’s services and your Apple ID settings. Backend requests may be retained only as needed to operate, debug, protect, and improve the catalog and scanning services. Children AFT is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Your Choices You can delete figures, photos, and other collection data inside the app. You can uninstall the app to remove local app data from your device. You can manage iCloud data and advertising privacy settings through iOS Settings. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
