ANIME FIGURE TRACKER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Anime Figure Tracker (“AFT,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”) helps users identify anime figures, manage a personal collection, submit community ratings and photos, and optionally publish a public collection gallery. This policy explains what information is processed, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Anime Figure Tracker iOS app, its catalog and identification services, public collection galleries, community features, support services, and related websites.

2. Information stored on your device

Most collection information is stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • Figure names, characters, series, manufacturers, lines and scales;
  • Purchase prices, retail prices and currency preferences;
  • Personal notes, conditions, statuses and shelf locations;
  • Photos of your figures and figure boxes;
  • Featured-figure selections and display preferences;
  • App theme, layout and filter preferences; and
  • Subscription-entitlement status.

This locally stored collection is not automatically uploaded as a complete collection. Information is sent off-device only when needed for a feature you use, such as figure identification, community submissions or publishing a gallery.

3. Figure scanning and identification

When you scan a box, barcode or figure, AFT may process:

  • A resized and compressed camera image;
  • A barcode detected in the image;
  • Text detected through optical character recognition;
  • Possible character, series, manufacturer and product names;
  • Search queries derived from the detected information; and
  • Basic request information needed for security and rate limiting.

This information is used to search the AFT catalog and, when necessary, request results from configured barcode, retailer, search or artificial intelligence providers.

AFT does not intentionally retain ordinary scan images after the identification request has been completed. Third-party providers may process request information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Sign in with Apple and gallery accounts

Signing in is optional and is only required for hosted public-gallery features.

AFT uses Sign in with Apple to authenticate gallery accounts. We do not request your Apple name or email address. We receive an Apple-provided user identifier and store it as a one-way hash. This identifier is used to recognize your gallery account on future sign-ins.

AFT also creates:

  • An internal gallery-account identifier;
  • A secure session token stored in the iOS Keychain;
  • A hashed copy of the session token on the server; and
  • A session expiration date. Gallery sessions normally expire after 90 days.

The gallery account identifier is linked to the gallery information and optional gallery photos you publish. It is not provided to advertising partners and is not used to track you across apps or websites.

5. Public collection galleries

Publishing a gallery is optional. Gallery sharing and collection-value sharing are turned off by default.

When you publish a gallery, AFT may store:

  • Your chosen gallery title and theme;
  • The number of figures in the gallery;
  • Figure names, characters, series, manufacturers, lines, scales and featured rankings;
  • Your total collection value, but only if you turn on “Show total collection value”; and
  • Resized and compressed gallery copies of figure photos, but only if you turn on “Include figure photos.”

Gallery publishing does not include individual purchase prices, personal notes, barcodes, local photo filenames or shelf locations. Your original full-resolution photos are not uploaded as gallery photos.

A published gallery receives a public link. Anyone who obtains that link may view and reshare its public content. AFT instructs search engines not to index public galleries, but we cannot guarantee that a shared link will remain private or will never be copied by another person.

Unpublishing a gallery

Selecting “Unpublish” makes the public link unavailable. Gallery information may remain stored so that you can republish the gallery using the same account and link.

Turning off “Include figure photos” and updating the gallery removes its hosted gallery-photo copies. Deleting your gallery account deletes the account, active sessions, public-gallery metadata and associated hosted gallery photos.

6. Community ratings

If you submit a Community Aura rating, AFT processes:

  • The catalog figure being rated;
  • Your rating from 1 through 5; and
  • A one-way hash derived from a random per-install identifier to prevent duplicate ratings.

Community ratings are displayed publicly as an aggregate average and review count. Your gallery account, Apple identity and personal collection are not shown with the rating.

7. Community photo submissions

If you voluntarily submit a photo for the community catalog, AFT may store:

  • The submitted figure photo;
  • Associated figure and catalog information;
  • A pseudonymous per-install identifier;
  • The date and text of your consent; and
  • The submission’s review status and reviewer notes.

Community photos are reviewed before approval. An approved photo may be displayed to other users as a community catalog image. A rejected photo will not be published.

Only submit photos you took yourself or otherwise have the right to share. Do not submit photos containing faces, addresses, receipts, account information or other sensitive information.

8. Purchases and subscriptions

AFT Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. AFT does not receive or store your payment-card or bank-account information.

The app receives subscription and entitlement information needed to determine whether Pro features are active. Apple handles billing, renewal, cancellation and payment processing under Apple’s own privacy policy and terms.

9. Advertising

The free version of AFT may display advertising supplied by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers;
  • Approximate or coarse location;
  • Advertising interactions and product interactions;
  • Advertising data;
  • IP address and device information;
  • Crash, performance and diagnostic data; and
  • Consent and privacy-choice information.

The exact information used may depend on your location, device settings, Apple permissions and choices made through the consent form. Google may use this information for ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, analytics and, where permitted, personalized advertising.

AFT does not provide your Sign in with Apple gallery identifier, gallery photos or gallery metadata to Google for advertising or cross-app tracking.

10. Technical and diagnostic information

AFT and its service providers may process limited technical information needed to operate and protect the service, including:

  • Request timestamps and response status;
  • IP address and general network information;
  • App and operating-system version;
  • Crash and performance information;
  • Rate-limit and abuse-prevention information; and
  • Errors generated while using a network feature.

Cloudflare may process IP addresses and request information while delivering and protecting AFT’s backend services. AFT uses IP information for security and rate limiting and does not intentionally attach it to your gallery profile.

11. How information is used

Information is processed to:

  • Identify figures and return catalog results;
  • Provide barcode and text-based searches;
  • Maintain and improve the figure catalog;
  • Authenticate gallery accounts;
  • Publish, update and unpublish collection galleries;
  • Store optional gallery photos;
  • Provide community ratings and reviewed community images;
  • Verify subscription entitlements;
  • Display and measure advertising;
  • Prevent duplicate submissions, fraud, abuse and excessive usage;
  • Diagnose errors and maintain service reliability; and
  • Respond to support, privacy and deletion requests.

13. Sale and sharing of information

AFT does not sell your gallery account, collection information or gallery photos for money.

Information may be disclosed:

  • At your direction, such as when you publish or share a gallery;
  • To the service providers described in this policy;
  • To comply with law, legal process or enforceable government requests;
  • To investigate fraud, abuse or security incidents; or
  • To protect the rights, safety and integrity of AFT and its users.

Advertising providers may process or share advertising information as described in their own policies and according to your consent and device settings.

14. Data retention

Retention depends on the feature:

  • On-device collection: retained until you edit or delete it, delete the app, or erase the device data.
  • Identification requests: ordinary scan images are not intentionally retained by AFT after processing.
  • Gallery account: retained until you delete the account.
  • Gallery sessions: normally expire after 90 days and are replaced when you sign in again.
  • Published gallery: retained until updated or deleted. Unpublishing disables public access but may retain the gallery for later republication.
  • Gallery photos: retained while included in the gallery or until removed through an update or account deletion.
  • Community ratings: may be retained to preserve the accuracy of aggregate ratings.
  • Community submissions: may be retained for review, catalog integrity, moderation and abuse prevention.
  • Security and diagnostic information: retained only as reasonably needed for security, troubleshooting and legal obligations.

Service providers may maintain backups or records for limited periods under their own retention requirements.

15. Your choices and controls

You can:

  • Decline camera or photo-library access;
  • Change camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings;
  • Use the app without creating a gallery account;
  • Keep collection value and gallery photos private;
  • Update or unpublish your public gallery;
  • Sign out of your gallery account;
  • Delete the hosted gallery account from the gallery screen using “Delete Account”;
  • Delete locally stored figures and photos within the app;
  • Manage or cancel AFT Pro through your Apple subscription settings;
  • Change advertising consent choices where the consent form is available;
  • Limit ad tracking through Apple’s privacy settings; and
  • Contact us to ask a privacy or deletion question.

Signing out removes the local gallery session but does not delete the hosted account. Use “Delete Account” when you want the hosted gallery account and its associated data deleted.

16. Security

AFT uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect information, including HTTPS encryption in transit, hashed account identifiers, hashed server-side session tokens, iOS Keychain storage, access controls, upload-size limits and non-indexed public-gallery links.

No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Public gallery information should be treated as public once you share its link.

17. International processing

AFT and its providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different privacy laws. We use service providers with safeguards intended to protect information during international processing.

18. Children’s privacy

AFT is not directed to children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

19. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, or to object to certain processing.

Many requests can be completed directly in the app. You may also contact us using the support link below. We may need to verify that a request relates to your account before completing it.

20. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when AFT adds features, changes service providers or must address legal or operational requirements. The “Last updated” date will be changed when the policy is revised. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or through the App Store listing.
