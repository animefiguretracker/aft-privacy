Privacy Policy for Anime Figure Tracker Effective Date: August 29, 2026 Anime Figure Tracker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how that information is used, and what choices you have. If you have questions or requests, contact us at: animefiguretracker@gmail.com 1. Information We Collect Collection Data You may enter information about your anime figure collection, including figure names, series, characters, manufacturers, product lines, release dates, prices, condition, shelf location, notes, wishlist status, preorder status, and other collection details. Private Photos You may add photos to figures in your personal collection. These photos are stored on your device unless you choose to submit a photo for community catalog review. Community Photo Contributions Anime Figure Tracker lets users optionally submit their own figure photos to help improve the community catalog. Photo submission is opt-in. We do not upload your collection photos for community catalog use unless you choose to share a photo for catalog review. When you opt in, we may collect and process the submitted image, figure name, character, series, manufacturer, product line, JAN/barcode if available, consent timestamp, and a device-generated identifier used to associate approved contribution counts with secret-card rewards. Submitted photos are stored on our backend and held for manual review before they are used in the catalog. Photos may be approved, rejected, or removed. Approved photos may be displayed as community catalog images for other users. Rejected photos are not used as catalog images. You should only submit photos that you own or have permission to share. Do not submit store images, auction images, screenshots, copyrighted product photos from other websites, or photos containing private, offensive, or unrelated content. Device Identifier The app may generate a random device identifier. This identifier is used for catalog matching, abuse prevention, contribution tracking, and approved community photo reward counts. It is not the advertising identifier and is not intended to personally identify you. Purchases If you purchase AFT Pro, Apple processes the transaction. Anime Figure Tracker may check your purchase status using Apple’s StoreKit services to unlock paid features. Advertising and Consent Anime Figure Tracker may use Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage advertising consent where required. These services may collect or process advertising-related data according to their own policies and your consent choices. Diagnostics and Analytics Apple and third-party services may provide diagnostic, crash, performance, or usage information depending on your device settings and consent choices. 2. How We Use Information We use information to: • Save and display your figure collection. • Identify figures using barcode scanning, OCR, image lookup, and catalog search. • Improve catalog matching and figure recognition. • Store and review opt-in community photo submissions. • Display approved community photos in the catalog. • Track approved contribution counts for Pro secret-card rewards. • Provide AFT Pro features. • Show ads where applicable. • Prevent abuse, fraud, spam, copied image submissions, and misuse. • Respond to support requests. • Maintain and improve app performance and reliability. 3. Community Photo Review Community photo submission is optional. If you submit a photo for catalog review, you confirm that you own the photo or have permission to share it. Submitted photos may be manually reviewed before they are approved for catalog use. We may reject or remove submitted photos for any reason, including if a photo appears copied, unclear, unrelated, offensive, private, misleading, copyrighted, or otherwise unsuitable for the catalog. Approved photos may be shown to other users as part of the community catalog. 4. Data Sharing We do not sell your personal information. We may share or process data with service providers that help operate the app, including: • Cloudflare, for backend hosting, database storage, image submission storage, and admin review tools. • Apple, for purchases, App Store services, diagnostics, and platform features. • Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform, for ads and consent management where applicable. • Backend catalog or recognition services used to identify figures and improve catalog search. Service providers may process data only as needed to provide their services. 5. Data Storage and Retention Collection data is primarily stored on your device and may sync through Apple services depending on your device settings and app configuration. Private collection photos are stored on your device unless you choose to submit a photo for community catalog review. If you delete the app, private on-device collection photos are normally deleted with the app data. Opt-in community photo submissions are different. If you choose to submit a photo for community catalog review, a copy of that submitted photo and related figure metadata is uploaded to our backend. Deleting the app does not automatically delete photos that were already submitted to our backend. Pending community photos may be stored while awaiting review. Approved community photos may remain in the community catalog and may be shown to other users as catalog images unless removed by us or unless you request deletion. Rejected photos may be retained for a limited time for moderation, abuse prevention, audit, or support purposes, then deleted or de-identified when no longer needed. Because Anime Figure Tracker does not require user accounts, we may need reasonable identifying details to process deletion requests, such as the figure name, approximate submission date, JAN/barcode if available, and any other information that helps us locate the submitted photo. 6. Your Choices You can choose not to submit community photos. You can remove photos from your personal collection in the app. You can revoke consent or request deletion of submitted community photos by contacting: animefiguretracker@gmail.com Please include enough information for us to identify the submitted photo, such as the figure name, approximate submission date, JAN/barcode if available, and any related details. If you delete Anime Figure Tracker from your device, your private on-device app data may be deleted from that device. However, deleting the app does not automatically notify us or delete opt-in community photos that were previously uploaded to our backend. To request deletion of previously submitted community photos, contact us at the email address above. 7. Children’s Privacy Anime Figure Tracker is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it. 8. User Content Rules By submitting a photo for catalog review, you agree that: • You took the photo yourself or have permission to share it. • You grant Anime Figure Tracker permission to store, review, resize, display, and use the photo as part of the community catalog. • You will not submit images copied from stores, auctions, databases, social media, other collectors, or copyrighted websites. • You will not submit offensive, explicit, private, misleading, unrelated, or illegal content. • We may approve, reject, remove, or stop displaying any submitted photo at any time. • We may revoke contribution rewards if submissions are abusive, copied, fraudulent, or submitted in bad faith. 9. Security We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. 10. International Processing Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Service providers may process information according to their own infrastructure and legal obligations. 11. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and may provide additional notice where appropriate. 12. Contact For privacy questions, deletion requests, or support, contact: animefiguretracker@gmail.com
